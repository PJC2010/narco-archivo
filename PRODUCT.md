# NarcoHistoria
A personal, source-backed digital compendium of Mexican organized-crime history and its present-day reporting. The owner explores and maintains people, cartels, factions, armed wings and relationships through profiles and dated hierarchical trees. Upload images with provenance, write narratives, attach citations, and edit every record without code. Mobile and desktop web are equally important.

## Confirmed scope
Confirmed: public readers with owner-only editing; English interface; empty archive for the owner’s research. Present-day reporting is shown with source dates, never implied live. Allegations, convictions, historical roles, disputed identities and uncertain relationships are separately labeled. No invented portraits or operational location tracking.

## Success
Persistent records and photos; usable responsive tree; search and filters; accessible profile/editor flows; visible provenance and source links; backup export.
