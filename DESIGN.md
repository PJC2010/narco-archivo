# NarcoHistoria visual system
Research journal for an owner-operated public historical archive. Quiet pale green navigation and white reading surfaces, forest green actions, Source Serif 4 titles and Source Sans 3 controls. Dense records are ruled rows. Tree nodes use a connected diagram on desktop and a vertical nested outline on mobile. No factual records or portraits are seeded. Evidence states are labeled in words as well as color.

Public readers browse records, hierarchical relationships and sources. Owner workspace follows the same frame with draft/published status and a focused full-width editor. Every image retains its original caption and source attribution.
